# Threat-to-Inference Matrix

| Threat | Where it matters | Main response | Failure condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Broadband targeted to declining/growing places | Field Study 2 | funded vs rejected applicants; score/cutoff design if recoverable; fixed effects | treatment predicts pre-trends |
| National occupation trends | Field Studies 2/4 | occupation × year fixed effects | results vanish under occupation-time controls |
| Local macro shocks | Field Studies 2/4 | geography × year fixed effects | effect appears equally in nontradable occupations |
| Pre-existing agglomeration | Field Study 2 | pre-trend event study; pre-treatment advantage fixed before treatment | treatment effect starts before rollout |
| SUTVA/spillovers | Broadband/platform studies | market-level RCT; competitor-exposure mapping; broader geography | estimated sign changes under reasonable exposure definitions |
| Occupation-code changes | OEWS/IPUMS | deterministic crosswalk; stable occupation subset | result depends on unstable mappings |
| Metro-boundary changes | OEWS | consistent geography/county crosswalk | result depends on one boundary vintage |
| Suppressed cells | OEWS | no primary imputation; bounds/robustness | result requires aggressive imputation |
| AI exposure measurement error | AI study | multiple independent pre-treatment measures | result appears only under one LLM-generated index |
| Post-2022 macro slowdown | AI study | long pre-trends; occupation-time/geography-time controls; actual adoption if possible | divergence predates AI |
| Pandemic remote-work shock | AI study | separate 2020-2021 transition; robustness excluding transition period | effect driven entirely by remote-work occupations before 2022 |
| General automation | Both waves | routine-task controls and negative-control occupations | market-shelter variables add no explanatory content |
| Selection into experimental markets | RCT | random assignment after recruitment and skill measurement | differential attrition after assignment |
| Demand expansion confounded with integration | RCT | fixed buyer-to-seller ratio and aggregate budget | total effective demand differs by arm |
| Superstar effect confounded with quality | RCT | frozen pre-treatment skill and identical task pools | producer quality distribution differs by arm |
| Researcher degrees of freedom | All | preregistration, frozen crosswalks, public decision log | primary specification chosen after outcomes |
