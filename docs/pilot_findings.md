# Pilot descriptive findings

Status: verified descriptive benchmark only. These results are not causal evidence for the local-scarcity mechanism.

## Data

U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics Industry Productivity employment series accessed through FRED.

Series used:

- Newspaper Publishers: IPUJN51111W200000000
- Newspaper, Periodical, Book, and Directory Publishers: IPUJN5111W200000000
- Independent Artists, Writers, and Performers: IPUSN7115W200000000

All are annual and measured in thousands of jobs.

## Benchmark changes

| Series | 2001 | 2025 | Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Newspaper publishers | 435.0 | 82.2 | -81.1% |
| Newspaper, periodical, book, and directory publishers | 805.8 | 229.5 | -71.5% |
| Independent artists, writers, and performers | 310.7 | 434.5 | +39.8% |

## Annualized log-linear employment trends

| Series | 1987-2000 | 2001-2019 | 2001-2025 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Newspaper publishers | -0.51% | -6.21% | -7.00% |
| Publishing aggregate | +0.15% | -4.88% | -5.33% |
| Independent artists, writers, and performers | +1.46% | -0.35% | +0.69% |

## Interpretation permitted by these data

The figures establish a sharp divergence between legacy publishing institutions and employment classified in independent artistic, writing, and performance activity.

They are consistent with a story of institutional reorganization rather than a simple disappearance of creative work.

## Interpretation not permitted by these data

These series do not establish that the internet caused the publishing decline.

They do not measure local geographic scarcity.

They do not identify whether workers moved between industries.

They do not measure creator earnings concentration.

They do not establish welfare effects.

They do not identify the counterfactual number of jobs absent digitization.

They do not show whether the rise in independent work reflects high-quality livelihoods.

## Role in the paper

Use these facts as motivation and as a descriptive contrast.

Do not use the 2001 breakpoint as a causal treatment date.

The causal Wave 1 evidence must come from geographic variation in digital connectivity interacted with predetermined occupation-level tradability.
