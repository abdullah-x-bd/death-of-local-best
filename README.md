# From Local Scarcity to Machine Abundance

**Digital Markets and the Erosion of Competence Rents**

This repository contains the reproducible research workflow for a paper studying how successive technologies alter the economic value of locally scarce human competence.

## Core research question

Did digital market integration erode geographic scarcity rents for occupations whose outputs can travel over distance, and does generative AI represent a second shock by reducing the scarcity of competent cognitive output itself?

## Epistemic structure

The project separates claims into four categories:

1. **Descriptive facts** documented directly from harmonized data.
2. **Causal estimates** supported by explicit identification assumptions and falsification tests.
3. **Mechanism evidence** imported from credible experimental or quasi-experimental research.
4. **Hypotheses and extrapolations** labeled as such.

No descriptive association will be presented as causal evidence.

## Planned empirical architecture

### Wave 1: digital market integration

Primary unit: geography × occupation × year.

Primary mechanism: declining geographic market protection for occupations with high digital tradability.

Primary outcomes:
- occupational geographic ubiquity
- local employment share
- real earnings

Secondary outcomes:
- geographic concentration
- self-employment
- occupational entry
- migration
- earnings dispersion
- disappearance of local occupational niches

### Wave 2: generative AI

The AI section will distinguish:
- causally identified productivity and skill-compression evidence from experiments and workplace studies
- early observational labor-market evidence
- predictions generated by the competence-rent framework

The paper will not infer economy-wide causal displacement from a simple post-2022 comparison.

## Reproducibility

The analysis is being built in R with scripted data acquisition, deterministic crosswalks, explicit decision logs, versioned dependencies, and prespecified primary specifications.

## Status

Research build in progress. Primary outcome regressions are intentionally deferred until the identification design, treatment construction, harmonization rules, and analysis plan are frozen.
